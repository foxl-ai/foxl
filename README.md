## It just works. All day. Every day.

Foxl is a personal AI agent that lives in your menu bar. Ask it anything. It drives your real Chrome, writes code, manages files, and runs schedules while you sleep. One click from recent chats. No browser tab. No separate account. No prompt engineering.

## Powered by the best models.

Claude Opus 4.7 and Sonnet 4.6 out of the box, with adaptive extended thinking. Bring your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Ollama, Claude Pro/Max, or ChatGPT Plus. Switch mid-conversation.

## Real browser. Not a clone.

Foxl drives the Chrome you already use — with your cookies, your logins, your extensions. Book a flight. File a form. Pull a report from an internal dashboard. No 2FA friction. No scraping tricks.

## Remembers you.

A curated memory that builds over time. Your role, your projects, your preferences, what worked, what didn't. Durable across updates. Human-readable. Yours.

## Runs while you're away.

Cron, heartbeats, webhooks. Daily briefings at 7 AM. Nightly backups. Weekly audits. All in natural language. All from one sidebar.

## Spawns help when it needs it.

Up to five subagents in parallel. Research one angle, code another, write a third. Foxl merges the results and hands you a summary.

## Built for macOS and Windows.

Universal builds for Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed installers for Windows 10 and later. Auto-updates. Launches at login. Stays out of the way until you need it.
